Global Ads Performance Dashboard

Question: across ₹1.11 Cr of spend on Google, Meta and TikTok, which platform actually delivers?

Finding: Google takes about 57% of spend yet has the lowest ROAS (4.1x) and the highest cost per acquisition (₹64.06). TikTok returns 9.5x ROAS at ₹29.20 CPA, with Meta between the two. The pattern holds across all 7 countries.

Recommendation: move incremental budget towards TikTok, starting with Search campaigns, after validating conversion volume and attribution.
